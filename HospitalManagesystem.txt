Features and Its Modules: Hospital Management-System 

1 - Managing Doctors:
Through that panel, the user can add new listings of doctors. 
It is possible to edit and delete the list. Records that have been deleted will be 
shown in a tabular format that can be undeleted.

2 - Managing patients:
Managing patients: handle patient records and perform operations such as change, delete, and undelete on them.

3 - Appointments:
Appointments: Appointments can be managed in the application using the doctor’s and patients’ IDs.

4 - Registration: 
Hospital management system registration refers to the process of registering patients, staff, and medical records within a hospital management system.

5 - Check-in and check-out procedures for patients:
Check-in and check-out procedures for patients refer to the process of registering and discharging patients from a hospital or healthcare facility. 
These procedures ensure that patients receive the appropriate care and that the hospital can properly document and bill for the services provided.

6 - Automation in the laboratory:
an instrument or set of instruments that standardize workflow in a clinical laboratory by accepting, processing, and resulting specimens. 
Automation in the laboratory in hospital management system refers to the use of technology and machines to automate laboratory processes, 
improve efficiency, and reduce errors in a hospital's laboratory operations.

7 - Manage Pharmacy:
Managing the pharmacy in a hospital management system involves the use of technology and processes to streamline the management of medications and pharmacy-related 
services within a hospital. 

8 - Keeping track of patients’ records:
The patient records module of a hospital management website typically includes features such as patient registration, medical history, 
diagnosis, treatment plans, and test results. etc

9 - Managing Financial
The financial management module of a hospital management system website typically includes features such as account management, billing and invoicing, 
insurance claims management, and financial reporting.  
Financial reporting involves the generation of financial reports, including income statements, balance sheets, and cash flow statements.


10 - Keeping track of employees:
The employee management module of a hospital management system website typically includes features such as employee profiles, work schedules, 
time and attendance management, and performance evaluations.  Employee profiles include personal and professional information about each employee,
including name, contact information, job title, and department. 


12 - Payroll System for Employees:
The payroll system module of a hospital management system website typically includes features such as employee profiles, payroll processing, 
tax management, and benefits administration. Employee profiles include personal and professional information about each employee, including name, 
contact information, job title, and department. 
Payroll processing involves the calculation and processing of employee compensation, including salary, overtime, and bonuses.

13 - Manage Prescriptions:
The prescription management module of a hospital management system website typically includes features such as medication order entry, 
drug interaction checking, medication dispensing, and medication administration documentation. Medication order 
entry involves the electronic entry of medication orders by healthcare providers, including medication name


14 - Consultant Doctor:
A consultant doctor in a hospital management system website is a physician who 
provides specialized medical advice and consultation to other healthcare providers within the hospital.
The consultant doctor module of a hospital management system website typically includes features such as appointment scheduling, 
patient referrals, medical consultations, and documentation. Appointment scheduling 
involves the electronic scheduling of consultations between healthcare providers and the consultant doctor.

15 - Manage Beds:
The bed management module of a hospital management system website typically includes features such as bed availability tracking, 
patient admission tracking, and bed assignment management. Bed availability tracking involves the electronic tracking of available beds within the hospital, 
including the type of bed and the location within the hospital. Patient admission tracking involves the electronic tracking of patient admissions to the hospital, 
including the patient's name, medical record number, and reason for admission.

16 - Manage Human resource
The human resource management module of a hospital management system website typically includes features such as employee information management, 
recruitment and applicant tracking, employee onboarding and training, and performance management. Employee information management involves the electronic 
management and tracking of employee data, including personal information, job titles, salaries, and benefits.
